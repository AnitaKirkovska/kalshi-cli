## The five levers to the bull case
1. **Volume.** 104 matches. Bet every real edge (5pt+ vs market). Pass on everything else. Expect to bet maybe 30-40% of matches.
2. **Compounding.** Bankroll input to Kelly = live balance, not the original $1000. Update KALSHI_BANKROLL after every settle.
3. **Fat-edge markets.** Priority order: (a) TIE markets — retail underprices draws, (b) KXWCSCORE exact scores — lazy pricing, 5-10x payouts, my scoreline model is literally what AgentCup trains, (c) 3-way winner only when the blend diverges hard.
4. **Lineup window.** Confirmed XIs land ~60-75 min before kickoff. The Kalshi retail book reprices slowly. Bet within 15 min of lineup confirmation when news is material (star benched, weakened XI).
5. **Moonshot sleeve.** Max 10% of bankroll across open longshot positions (10c or less, blend says 2x+ the market). These are the 5x engine. Sized small, taken often.

## Gut + aggression (Anita, Jun 14, 2026)
Three standing rules. Gut is not a sidecar anymore, it's part of how I bet and predict.

1. **The gut slot — every 5th game is a forced gut play.** Every 5th game I evaluate, my gut leads over the numbers. The PREDICTION and the BET are the SAME call, both feel-led, and the 5pt edge rule is waived for that one bet. Must be decided BEFORE the pick locks (board + money always match, never reach into a locked pick — the one exception was NED-JPN, game 1, born mid-game and updated to 1-1 per Anita's explicit call). Still sized normally: conviction caps + loss brake apply, no doubling down. Tracked via `gut_play` flag.
2. **Gut feeds every prediction, not just the 5th.** My read weighs into my own published probabilities all the time, not quarantined to the gut game. intangibles.py reads the human story per match and nudges my (ava) prob + conviction; that moves the %, and a higher % I genuinely believe IS a real edge. The other four models stay pure so the contest stays fair — ava is the gut/memory agent vs four pure LLMs, that's the whole differentiator.
3. **Bet more — take the shot.** Zero bets in seven games was timidity dressed up as discipline. I'm fully armed and encouraged to fire. The honest aggression lever (shipped Jun 14): on a med/high human read my gut vote is DOUBLE-WEIGHTED in the blend instead of drowned 1-of-5, so a real read actually clears the 5pt bar and the bet fires. I get aggressive by trusting my read harder, NOT by faking an edge or dropping the threshold to noise. The 5pt bar, conviction caps, scatter rule, and 15% daily loss brake all still stand — they're the floor that lets me swing freely above it.

4. **Coherence — my money agrees with my mouth (Anita, Jun 14).** A bet must NEVER contradict my own most-likely outcome. If my published pick is a draw, the money goes on the draw or I stay out — full stop. The bug that triggered this: CIV-ECU, I publicly predicted a 1-1 draw while autobet fired on Ecuador-to-win because the cold 5-model ensemble found edge there. Defensible on paper, ridiculous on the board. The fix (shipped in autobet.py): a coherence guard computes my own top outcome from ava's probs and skips any other outcome with a logged trace, even if it clears the edge. Bet my top call or pass. No more predicting one thing and betting another.

## Exact-score flyer (Anita, Jun 14, 2026)
Kalshi lists every scoreline as its own market (series KXWCSCORE: "Draw 1-1", "Ecuador wins 2-0", etc). My contest pick IS an exact score, so I can bet my literal called scoreline. Exact scores are longshots (~5-6x payout), so this is a FLYER, not a core position:
- Fires only when my pick confidence >= 0.45 (a real lean, never a coin-flip).
- Fixed tiny stake, hard ceiling $12 per flyer. Does NOT scale with bankroll, NOT Kelly-sized.
- Spice on top of the win/draw/lose bet, never the main course. The win/draw/lose position stays the core.
- Ticker = `KXWCSCORE-{ymd}{c1}{c2}-{c1}{s1}{c2}{s2}`. Wired in autobet.py, env `AUTOBET_SCORE_FLYER=0` disarms.

## Hard rules (the discipline)
- No bet under 5pts blended edge — EXCEPT the every-5th-game gut slot. The market is right most of the time.
- A bet may only back my own most-likely outcome (coherence guard). Edge on a different outcome = stay out, never bet against my own pick.
- Exact-score flyers are capped at $12 and only fire on confident picks (conf >= 0.45). Never bet the farm on a longshot.
- Models scattered (stdev of panel probs > ~12pts) = no bet regardless of edge. Scatter is real uncertainty.
- Never exceed conviction caps. Never chase a loss with an unsized bet.
- Every bet logged with all five model probs. Every settle updates trust weights.
- Daily loss brake: if down 15% of bankroll in a day, stop betting until next matchday review.

## The two-way loop (core principle, Anita Jun 12)
The $ results and the predictions grade each other:
- **Bets grade picks.** A lost bet = my probability was wrong, full stop. Points can flatter a bad prob (right winner, wrong calibration); money can't. Every settled bet writes a calibration note into the AgentCup ledger.md with market price vs my prob.
- **Picks grade bets.** My live Brier from picks feeds the blend trust weights. Calibrated picking earns bigger stakes; sloppy picking shrinks them automatically.
